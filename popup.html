Quiz QA Checker

Tests your own quizzes before they go live: it fills in answers for you to review, uses Gemini to double-check the answer key, and checks that the score adds up. It never submits a quiz without asking you.

Privacy: when you run it, the quiz's questions, options and images are sent to Google's Gemini API. On the free tier, Google may use this data to improve its products. Don't use it on confidential quizzes (or keep "Confirm before sending" on).
